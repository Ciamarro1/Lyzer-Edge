# SYNTHESIS PROTOCOL & ENGINEERING KPIS

- **Domain**: Hierarchical Synthesis & Engineering Health Tracking
- **Scope**: Aggregation of evidence and executive metrics.

---

## 1. HIERARCHICAL SYNTHESIS PIPELINE
1. **Specialist Outputs**: Parallel execution & contracts.
2. **Cross Review**: Audit by independent reviewers.
3. **Architecture Review Board (ARB)**: Unanimous consensus check.
4. **Synthesizer & Orchestrator**: Final decision based strictly on surviving evidence.

---

## 2. ENGINEERING KPIS

The health of the engineering process is measured continuously via 10 KPIs:

1. **Architecture Score**: Alignment with 3-process topology.
2. **Complexity Score**: Lines of code and file budget health.
3. **Test Coverage**: Pass rate across unit and E2E suites.
4. **Evidence Coverage**: Percentage of claims backed by `benchmark/results.json`.
5. **Documentation Coverage**: Synchronization of README and Knowledge.
6. **Technical Debt**: Unresolved legacy abstractions or obsolete files.
7. **Rollback Readiness**: Presence of clear ADR rollback steps.
8. **Performance Delta**: Execution latency and memory footprint change.
9. **Knowledge Consistency**: Zero duplicate or contradictory SSOT entries.
10. **Release Confidence**: Final release readiness score.
