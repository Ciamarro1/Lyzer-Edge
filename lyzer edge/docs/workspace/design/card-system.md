# LACW — Universal Card System Specification

## Universal Card Anatomy
Every Card inside LACW must answer 8 fundamental questions:
1. **Who am I?** (Entity ID & Title)
2. **Why do I exist?** (Operational Purpose)
3. **What do I represent?** (Reality Tag: Observed, Inferred, Synthetic)
4. **What is my state?** (Status: Nominal, Degraded, Vetoed)
5. **What is my confidence?** (Bayesian Confidence Score)
6. **What is my origin?** (Source Engine or Agent)
7. **What is my history?** (Version counter & audit trail link)
8. **What commands do I accept?** (Ctrl+K action triggers)
