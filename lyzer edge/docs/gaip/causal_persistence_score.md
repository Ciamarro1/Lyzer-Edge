# Causal Persistence Score (CPS) Specification

**Release:** 1.8.6-A (Ghost Alpha Investigation Program - GAIP)
**Entity:** Lyzer Labs

## 1. Overview

As explicitly mandated by the Central Intelligence Authority (CIA), the detection of Ghost Alpha decay must strictly avoid traditional financial performance metrics such as PnL, Drawdown, or Sharpe ratios. These metrics are lagging indicators susceptible to transient statistical noise and do not reflect the underlying structural validity of an alpha source.

Instead, the Ghost Alpha Investigation Program (GAIP) relies exclusively on **Causal Persistence Decay**. To quantify this decay, Lyzer Labs introduces the **Causal Persistence Score (CPS)**. The CPS is an epistemic and structural integrity metric designed to evaluate the continuous causal link between the initial alpha hypothesis and its ongoing manifestation.

## 2. The Four Pillars of the Causal Persistence Score

The CPS is formulated as a composite index of four orthogonal factors. Each factor measures a specific dimension of causal degradation.

### 2.1. Evidence Stability
**Definition:** The degree to which the foundational empirical evidence supporting the original alpha hypothesis remains robust, observable, and statistically significant.
- **Measurement Focus:** Tracks the continued presence of the specific market inefficiencies, data relationships, or signal-to-noise ratios that the alpha relies upon.
- **Decay Indicator:** A reduction in Evidence Stability indicates that the market structure has shifted or the inefficiency has been arbitraged away, rendering the core premise invalid, regardless of short-term PnL anomalies.

### 2.2. Authority Stability
**Definition:** The consistency, reliability, and operational fidelity of the infrastructure governing the alpha generation process.
- **Measurement Focus:** Evaluates the integrity of data pipelines, the structural validity of predictive models, and the precision of execution mechanisms against their original specifications.
- **Decay Indicator:** Degradation in Authority Stability implies an infrastructure failure or pipeline drift. The mechanism capturing the alpha is introducing systematic errors or noise, severing the causal link between the signal and the execution.

### 2.3. Regime Consistency
**Definition:** The structural alignment between the current macroeconomic and microstructural environment and the specific market regime under which the alpha hypothesis is causally effective.
- **Measurement Focus:** Compares real-time environmental factors (e.g., volatility clusters, liquidity profiles, cross-asset correlation matrices) against the alpha's designated operational regime.
- **Decay Indicator:** A divergence in Regime Consistency means the alpha is operating outside its designed parameters. Any performance generated in this state is non-causal and unpredictable, elevating the risk of sudden, catastrophic failure.

### 2.4. Observer Consistency
**Definition:** The stability of the internal assumptions, hyperparameters, and observational frameworks utilized by the system to evaluate and interact with the alpha.
- **Measurement Focus:** Monitors for "semantic drift" or implicit parameter instability. Ensures the system is not silently curve-fitting or altering its foundational logic to accommodate new, contradictory data.
- **Decay Indicator:** A breakdown in Observer Consistency signifies an epistemic failure. The system's internal perception of reality has shifted, compromising the objectivity of its evaluations and invalidating its causal assumptions.

## 3. Operational Implementation

The aggregate CPS determines the operational status of an alpha source:

*   **Continuous Monitoring:** The four factors must be evaluated continuously and independently.
*   **Decoupling from PnL:** A declining CPS necessitates immediate investigation and potential suspension of the alpha strategy, **even if recent financial performance appears strong.**
*   **Structural Truth:** By relying on the CPS, Lyzer Labs ensures that capital is allocated based on structural truth and causal validity, neutralizing the threat of Ghost Alpha.
