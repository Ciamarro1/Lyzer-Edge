# Observation Throughput Doctrine (OTD)

## Institutional Directive
**Status:** ACTIVE
**Architectural State:** FROZEN
**Primary Bottleneck:** Evidence Throughput

### 1. The Core Principle
The era of Architectural Building is suspended. The Lyzer Labs system has transitioned into the era of Evidence Acquisition. The primary threat to institutional integrity is no longer a lack of governance, but **Framework Proliferation**—the creation of structures faster than the reality they are meant to observe.

### 2. The 10:1 Ratio Rule
No new architectural layer, framework, or structural artifact may be proposed or discussed unless the system has acquired a minimum ratio of **10 New Observations per 1 New Artifact**.

*   **10 New Observations** → Authorizes the discussion of 1 minor structural artifact.
*   **100 New Observations** → Authorizes the discussion of relevant structural evolution (e.g., Release 1.9).

### 3. Execution Mandate
The sole legitimate vector of progress for Lyzer Labs is to increase `n` (Observation Count) via the established Observation Pipeline (Delta → Epsilon → Zeta → EVP). 
Every future observation will be processed blindly and logged in the Empirical Memory Layer (EML).

**Objective:** Maintain Falsification. Block Architecture Relapse. Force Reality Exposure.

### 4. Amendment: Evidence Diversity
Quantity alone is insufficient; diversity is required. Evidence must span multiple operational conditions, failure modes, and structural variations. 
**Crucial Distinction:** Phenotypic classes are METADATA ONLY. They serve as descriptive tags for organizational grouping, not as causal explanations for system behavior.

### 5. Amendment: Continuous Reality Stress (CRS)
The Observation Pipeline transitions from discrete batch extraction to **Windowed Streaming** — continuous, overlapping ingestion windows without cognitive pauses between runs.

**CRS Definition:** An empirical probe designed to locate the Cognitive Saturation Boundary (CSB) — the point where continuous observation inevitably produces the first interpretation event.

**CRS is NOT:**
* A permanent operational regime
* A test of infinite resistance
* A framework for understanding data

**CRS IS:**
* A time-bounded experiment to discover Δt (epistemic resistance)
* Active until FIEL fires (first operation outside CAO detected)

**Prohibitions during CRS:**
* No recalibration of REE
* No read-back of EML content (health checks excepted)
* No addition to CAO
* No new architectural layers, frameworks, or structural artifacts
