# Reality Exposure Engine (REE) Specification

## 1. Core Definition
The Reality Exposure Engine (REE) is a pre-pipeline mechanical sampling filter designed for Lyzer Labs' quantitative intelligence ecosystem.

## 2. Primary Objective
REE operates strictly by adjusting sampling weights across EDC (Event-Driven Context) Classes A-G. 
- **Penalize:** Overrepresented regimes.
- **Boost:** Underrepresented regimes.

By maintaining representational equilibrium, REE ensures that downstream pipelines are exposed to a balanced reality spectrum, preventing statistical bias toward high-frequency but low-information market states.

## 3. CIA Strict Prohibitions
To ensure anti-fragility and maintain strict boundaries within the architectural framework, REE is subject to the following absolute prohibitions:

- **No Learning:** REE does not learn from data. It contains no stateful learning algorithms, neural networks, or parameter optimization based on feedback.
- **No Prediction:** REE makes zero predictions about future states or regimes.
- **No Signal Generation:** REE is strictly a filter. It generates zero trading, execution, or analytical signals.
- **Blindly Mechanical:** It operates exclusively as a blindly mechanical mechanism based on strict, predefined mathematical thresholds.

> **Fundamental Axiom:** *"If it starts looking intelligent, it has failed."*
