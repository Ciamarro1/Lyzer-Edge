# Evidence Diversity Campaign (EDC) Taxonomy

## Constitutional Warning
**WARNING:** This taxonomy is not an explanation. It is purely an organizational bucket. **"Taxonomy Reification" is strictly forbidden.** Do not mistake these classifications for causal models.

## Taxonomy Classes (Observation Extraction Buckets)

### Class A: Liquidity Shock
Observations related to sudden evaporation of market depth, abnormal bid-ask spreads, and sudden liquidity withdrawal.

### Class B: Volatility Explosion
Observations capturing extreme, rapid spikes in price variance and market turbulence that break historical short-term boundaries.

### Class C: Slow Death
Observations of persistent, steady deterioration in price, volume, or fundamentals over an extended period without sudden violent shocks.

### Class D: False Panic
Observations where rapid sell-offs or alarms are quickly reversed, indicating a market overreaction or stop-loss cascade rather than a structural failure.

### Class E: Regime Transition
Observations of structural shifts in market behavior, where historical correlations, volatility regimes, or established trading patterns permanently or durably alter.

### Class F: Macro Shock
Observations driven by exogenous events, such as central bank policy surprises, geopolitical shifts, or significant economic data deviations.

### Class G: Euphoria
Observations of rapid, potentially unsustainable price appreciation driven by momentum, extreme sentiment shifts, or leverage without immediate fundamental justification.
