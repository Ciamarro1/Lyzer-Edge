## Section 5: Conflicting ADRs

### 1. ADR-033 (Unification) vs. ADR-028 (Specialized Organism)
- **ADR-033 mandates** compressing all engines into ParametricDecisionEngine and GenericCompositeScore.
- **ADR-028 creates** highly specialized MarketEcologyEngine, StrategyCompetitionEngine, AlphaDecayEngine, StrategyMutationEngine -- exactly the kind of proliferation ADR-033 warns against.
- **Conflict**: ADR-028 was written before ADR-033, so ADR-033 supersedes it. ADR-028's specialized engines should be refactored into parametrized instances of the generic framework per ADR-033's mandate.

### 2. ADR-034 (UCC Meta-Governance) vs. ADR-040/041 (Widget Framework)
- **ADR-034 Rule**: No new module should be created if it can be expressed as $\langle \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{T}, \mathcal{M}, \mathcal{O} \rangle$.
- **ADR-040/041** created a full WidgetFramework (WidgetSDK, WidgetRegistry, WidgetLoader, StateStore, EventBus, TelemetryBus) as **new classes**, not as configurations of the existing CognitiveRuntime.
- **Conflict**: The Widget Framework introduced ~15 new classes that could arguably be expressed as plugins/policies on the existing CognitiveEventBus and DependencyContainer. ADR-041's Red Team review partly addressed this by switching from BaseWidget inheritance to IWidgetPlugin interface contract.

### 3. ADR-036 (Removal-First Law) vs. Expansion ADRs (025-032)
- **ADR-036 mandates**: "Every architectural proposal must first try to remove something before adding something."
- **ADR-025 through ADR-032** (Fases 8-15) added **8 new architectural layers** (cognitive-intelligence, empirical-validation, cognitive-portfolio, market-organism, cognitive-operations, distributed-runtime, institutional-production, utonomous-research) without removing any pre-existing layer.
- **Conflict**: Massive expansion phase violated the Removal-First Constitution. The codebase grew in complexity without corresponding pruning.

### 4. ADR-005 (Observability-First) vs. ADR-030 (Distributed Runtime)
- **ADR-005**: "Do not introduce microsecond HFT complexity before proving the real critical path bottleneck with empirical telemetry."
- **ADR-030**: Creates 6 specialized WorkerPoolEngine workers (MarketWorker, ResearchWorker, ReflectionWorker, EvolutionWorker, ValidationWorker, ExecutionWorker) with CognitiveEventBus backbone and DependencyContainer.
- **Conflict**: ADR-030 introduced significant distributed systems complexity (Event Bus, Workers, DI Container) **before** ADR-005's Phase 5.1 (Prometheus telemetry) was fully proven. The P99 latency bottleneck was never empirically validated before adding the worker pool architecture.

### 5. ADR-033 (No New Layers) vs. ADR-040/041/042 (Command Center Layer)
- **ADR-033 (Section 12)**: "The expansion of new layers in the Lyzer Edge is **terminated**. Evolution will be oriented by simplicity and parameter refinement."
- **ADR-040/041/042**: Created an entirely new **Command Center Layer** with Widget Framework, Dual-Pane Cockpit, and Provider Reality Boundary.
- **Conflict**: ADR-040 violated ADR-033's termination of new architectural layers. This suggests ADR-040 supersedes ADR-033's "no new layers" directive for the frontend domain.

## Summary

**Architectural Health**: The Lyzer Edge codebase has implemented 58% of all architectural decisions fully, with another 24% partially implemented. The Report ADRs (043-052) have 100% implementation rate, being the most recent and tightly focused. The Workspace ADRs (001-009) also have near-perfect implementation.

**Biggest Gap**: The **Lyzer Labs 1.7.X philosophical stack** (SIL through IVL) -- 7 ADRs representing a complete cognitive governance architecture -- remains entirely unimplemented. These were explicitly marked as "VISION ONLY" or "Proposed" and were superseded by the main architecture ADRs.

**Compression Debt**: The most impactful unrealized decisions are ADR-033's compression mandates (7 scores to 1, 15 engines to 1, 5 storage systems to 1). The codebase currently carries significant duplication that could be unified.

**Conflict Resolution Needed**: ADR-033 vs. ADR-028 (specialization vs. unification), ADR-034 vs. ADR-040 (meta-governance vs. widget framework expansion), and ADR-005 vs. ADR-030 (observability-first vs. distributed runtime) represent the three most significant unresolved architectural tensions.
