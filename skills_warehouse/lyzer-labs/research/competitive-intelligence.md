# Competitive Intelligence

> **Domain:** Research · Strategy
> **Agent:** All
> **Version:** 1.0.0

## Analysis Framework
1. Identify top competitors in niche
2. Analyze their content strategy (topics, keywords, formats)
3. Gap analysis — what they cover that we don't
4. Reverse engineer their traffic sources
5. Monitor their rank movements
6. Track their social media presence
7. Identify their affiliate partnerships

## Tools
- SEOScraperAgent (SERP scraping)
- CompetitorAnalysisAgent (gap analysis)
- RankTrackerAgent (position monitoring)
