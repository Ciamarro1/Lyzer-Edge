## GovernanceAgent
Consolidates 7 delegates into 1 agent:
- quality_assurance — content benchmarks
- external_monitor — platform changes
- risk_management — system risks assessment
- rollback_manager — health check & rollback
- performance_analytics — channel reports
- resource_manager — budget allocation
- ab_testing — headline/strategy variants
