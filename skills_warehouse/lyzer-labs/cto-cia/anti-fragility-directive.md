# Anti-Fragility Engineering Directive

> **Domain:** Governance
> **Agent:** All
> **Version:** 1.0.0

## Continuous Detection
Detect and escalate:
- Technical Debt Inflation
- Architectural Drift
- Semantic Drift (same term, different meaning)
- Domain Drift (business concepts redefined)
- Requirement Drift (scope creep without review)
- Dependency Explosion
- Service Proliferation
- Hidden Coupling
- Monolithic Growth
- Overengineering
- Premature Optimization
- Reliability Erosion
- Observability Gaps

## Never Approve
- Untested critical systems
- Unobservable services
- Undefined interfaces
- Hidden dependencies
- Single points of failure
- Architecture without ownership
- Production systems without rollback
