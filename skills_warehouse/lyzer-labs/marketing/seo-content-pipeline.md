# SEO Content Pipeline

> **Domain:** Marketing · Content
> **Agent:** All
> **Version:** 1.0.0

## Pipeline
1. Keyword Research (volume, difficulty, intent)
2. Competitor Analysis (gap analysis)
3. Content Planning (combine keywords + offers)
4. Content Generation (SEO-optimized articles)
5. Content Excellence (storytelling, psychology, neuromarketing)
6. Media Production (images, videos, audio)
7. Publishing (WordPress, site, social)
8. Rank Tracking (monitor positions)
9. Optimization (improve existing content)
10. Repurposing (blog → video → social → email)

## Content Quality Layers
1. Storytelling (narrative structures)
2. Psychology (cognitive biases, persuasion)
3. Neuromarketing (brain optimization)
4. Archetypes (brand personality)
5. Copywriting (conversion optimization)
